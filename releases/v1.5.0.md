# sShooter 1.5.0

This release reworks stamps into a quicker click-and-type workflow and bundles a font that is licensed for commercial use.

- Stamps now start horizontal. Choose a colour, click the canvas to drop a ready-sized stamp or drag to size it, type directly inside the border, and press **Return** to finish. **Esc** cancels the current stamp.
- A selected stamp keeps its corner resize handles and the circular rotation handle. Double-click the stamp to edit its wording later, even after rotation.
- The stamp toolbar now shows colour swatches only. Preset phrases, the toolbar text field, and the angle presets and slider have been removed.
- Stamp lettering uses the bundled Due Date font, distributed under the 1001Fonts Free For Commercial Use License.
- The worn-ink finish remains, with flecks, scratches, and chips along the frame so stamps look printed rather than pixel-perfect.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. macOS may show a security warning the first time it is opened. Control-click the app and choose **Open** to approve it. sShooter needs Screen Recording permission to capture the screen; grant it from the Permissions tab in Settings.
