# sShooter 1.2.0

This release adds a crop tool, so a capture can be trimmed before it is copied or saved.

- New **Crop** tool: click the crop button to trim the capture. The whole image stays visible with everything outside the frame dimmed, and the frame has handles on every corner and edge.
- Drag the frame to move it, drag a handle to resize it, or drag outside it to draw a new frame. Press **Return** to apply the crop or **Esc** to cancel it.
- The crop is non-destructive and undoable with **⌘Z**, so you can re-crop or bring the full image back at any time.
- Once a crop is applied, **Copy** and **Save** use the crop frame and anything drawn outside it is cut off; without a crop they still grow the output to fit every annotation as before.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. macOS may show a security warning the first time it is opened. Control-click the app and choose **Open** to approve it. sShooter needs Screen Recording permission to capture the screen; grant it from the Permissions tab in Settings.
