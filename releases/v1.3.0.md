# sShooter 1.3.0

This release refines the crop tool so it behaves the way a crop is expected to.

- Cropping is now trim-only. Once a crop is applied the discarded area is gone for good: re-opening the crop tool shows just the cropped region and lets you shrink it further, instead of revealing the whole capture again.
- While cropping, a short hint reminds you to press **Return** to apply the crop or **Esc** to cancel it.
- **⌘Z** still brings the full capture back, since the crop stays non-destructive under the hood.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. macOS may show a security warning the first time it is opened. Control-click the app and choose **Open** to approve it. sShooter needs Screen Recording permission to capture the screen; grant it from the Permissions tab in Settings.
